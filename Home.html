About Xpresso

Xpresso Café is a South African coffee-shop franchise that provides affordable coffee, beverages, snacks and freshly baked products to customers across the country. The business was founded in 2016 by Nicolene and Tomer Elhadad, with the aim of making quality coffee accessible to everyone. Since opening its first store in Durbanville, Cape Town, Xpresso Café has grown into a national franchise with more than 80 stores across South Africa. The brand is known for its affordable pricing, friendly atmosphere and wide range of coffee and food products.
