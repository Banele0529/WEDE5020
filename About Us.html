About Xpresso

Our story, vision, mission and values.

Our Story

Xpresso Café is a South African coffee franchise founded in 2016 by husband-and-wife entrepreneurs Nicolene and Tomer Elhadad. Their first store opened in Durbanville, Cape Town, with the aim of making quality coffee and treats affordable and accessible to everyone. The business originally sold every item on its menu for R10, which helped the brand quickly attract customers. Xpresso Café later expanded through franchising and grew into a national coffee brand with stores across South Africa. The founders' vision was to give people the opportunity to enjoy good-quality coffee without having to spend a lot of money.

Our Vision

Xpresso Café's official vision is to expand throughout South Africa and eventually across international borders. The organisation has stated an intention to open another 100 stores over a five-year period.

Our Mission

"To grow the coffee culture inside South Africa so that everyone can enjoy a great cup of coffee every single day."

Our Values

Quality

Providing quality coffee, food and treats.

Affordability

Making great coffee accessible to everyone.

Community
